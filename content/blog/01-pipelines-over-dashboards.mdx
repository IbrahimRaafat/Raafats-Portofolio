Pretty dashboards get the screenshots. Pipelines get the 3am pages.

At InfoMedia we were ingesting media signals from a dozen fragile sources — each with its own rate limit, pagination quirk, and way of failing silently. The original scraper reported success even when it returned half the dataset.

### What changed

- **Contract-first ingestion:** Every source now declares its expected shape with `zod`, fails loudly on drift.
- **Idempotent writes:** We can re-run yesterday's job without duplicating rows. Uses a stable `source_id + event_hash`.
- **Dead-letter visibility:** Instead of retrying forever, we park poison records in a DLQ table with the raw payload. Analysts can fix and replay.

Reliability went from ~60% to >95% not because the scraping got smarter, but because *failure became observable*.

> If you can't tell that you lost data, you will lose data.
